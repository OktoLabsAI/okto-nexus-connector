# M5k: release capacity after a proved initial lease refusal

A real Pi broadcast handoff produced an authenticated `LEASE_REVALIDATION_REQUIRED` receipt before native opening. The Server closed the one-shot session and reopened the handoff, but the Connector retained a consumed capacity reservation and its physical owned-slot placeholder. On reconnect, the general owned-slot fence found that placeholder and rejected reconciliation. The Connector retried the same recovery three times on each control connection and never became `CONTROL_READY`.

The Connector now releases this capacity at the live initial-refusal frontier. Replacement-owner reconciliation also scans retained owned slots before the general fence and releases only those whose exact initial lease refusal, immutable scope, Core no-effect receipt and absence of native claim/launch are durable. Missing or conflicting proof still blocks recovery. The Core capacity release itself refuses a slot whose native opening started or whose turn slot exists.

Fifteen focused tests passed, including live release and reconnect recovery with and without the durable Core fact. The modified Connector 0.0.10 wheel was installed into the isolated daemon environment. Without restarting the disposable Nexus at `127.0.0.1:8204` or editing its database, the daemon reconciled all 21 retained operations, returned `complete=true`, and reached `CONTROL_READY` on its first new control attempt. All four refused Pi opening reservations and their owned-slot placeholders were `RELEASED`; the later successful opening also released its slot.

In the real Nexus UI, the Pi connection still had a five-second one-shot start deadline, shorter than cold opening. Setting 300 seconds with the Connections form and Finish persisted the policy. After its bounded retry cooldown, the existing broadcast handoff was claimed again. The native Pi responded `M5J_RECOVERY_OK`, the one-shot call reached `SUCCEEDED`, and Handoffs displayed `COMPLETED` with that result. Its cold start took about 48 seconds. Connector control remained `CONTROL_READY` with zero consecutive control failures. This validates recovery and completion for this disposable remote Connector/Pi path; it does not qualify other harnesses or a separate host.
